# 🎯 Quick Feature Guide

## Your Portfolio Now Has:

### 🎬 Loading Screen
**What:** Animated loading screen with bouncing circles
**Where:** Shows when page first loads
**Duration:** 1.5 seconds
**Look for:** Cyan circles bouncing with "Loading Portfolio..." text

### ✨ Particle Background
**What:** 100 animated particles floating and connecting
**Where:** Entire background of the site
**Effect:** Particles draw lines when close to each other
**Look for:** Cyan dots moving slowly across the screen

### 🖱️ Cursor Trail
**What:** Glowing trail follows your mouse
**Where:** Anywhere you move your mouse (desktop only)
**Effect:** Small cyan circles fade out behind cursor
**Look for:** Move your mouse to see the trail

### 📬 Custom Notifications
**What:** Beautiful notification system (not boring alerts!)
**Where:** Top-right corner when you submit forms
**Effect:** Slides in from right, auto-dismisses after 4 seconds
**Try:** Submit the contact form to see it!

### 💧 Button Ripples
**What:** Material Design ripple effect
**Where:** All buttons (Download CV, Send Message, etc.)
**Effect:** Click creates expanding circle from click point
**Try:** Click any button!

### 🌊 Parallax Scrolling
**What:** Images move slower than content when scrolling
**Where:** Profile images in Home and About sections
**Effect:** Creates depth and 3D feel
**Try:** Scroll down and watch the images

### 📊 Animated Skill Bars
**What:** Skill bars fill up in sequence
**Where:** Skills section
**Effect:** Bars animate from 0% to target percentage
**Look for:** Scroll to skills section

### ✨ Hover Glow Effects
**What:** Elements glow when you hover over them
**Where:** Project cards, section headings, nav links
**Effect:** Cyan glow appears on hover
**Try:** Hover over any project card!
